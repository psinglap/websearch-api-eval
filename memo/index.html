The use case: a real-time GTM chat agent

For GTM agents that answer "what's new?" in a live conversation, You.com is the web search that is both fast enough for chat and fresh enough to trust. Its accuracy matched the other three.

  1. Fast enough for chat: 10 of 10 searches under 1 s; no other API managed more than 3 of 10.
  2. The freshest signals: 75% of results inside the requested window, and the only API to surface today's Apache Iggy activity.
  3. Never comes back empty: results on all 30 calls, against 9 empty for Tavily and 3 for Exa.

Parallel and Tavily did better for questions that need an official page, such as release notes or product docs.

Quick glance on queries Tested:

A sales rep or founder at company called "LaserData - selling to Apache Iggy developers" asks the agent in chat, for example in Slack or the product, "What's new at Confluent (competitor) today?" or "Who's talking about Apache Iggy?"
